# Healthcare Interoperability Lab (NutriTrack x HealthMap)

NutriTrack x HealthMap is an experimental healthcare interoperability project designed to bridge nutrition monitoring platforms and geographic health tracking systems through a shared standardized data pipeline.

## Problem & Motivation

Healthcare applications frequently store malnutrition metrics in isolated proprietary structures. Public health officers cannot cross-correlate nutritional deficiencies with regional demographics without manual data export and reconciliation.

## Architecture

The system coordinates two independent subsystems:
- **NutriTrack Service:** Handles patient intake, anthropometric measurements (weight, height, age), and growth chart calculations.
- **HealthMap Service:** Visualizes spatial distribution of nutritional status across health districts.
- **Interoperability Gateway:** Transforms internal records into standardized FHIR-compliant payloads (`Patient` and `Observation` resources) for synchronization.

## What Went Wrong & Debugging Stories

During early integration testing, concurrent webhook syncs caused duplicate observation entries in the analytics store due to race conditions during network latency spikes.

**Solution:** Implemented idempotency keys on every ingestion payload and structured transactional upsert logic in PostgreSQL, ensuring that retransmitted packets safely resolve without duplicated clinical observations.
